## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes commute times and traffic congestion patterns across major Lagos corridors between 2022 and 2025. The objective is to identify congestion hotspots, peak travel periods, and operational factors affecting travel times.
---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Cities often lack actionable insights into traffic and commuting behavior. This project aims to provide data-driven insights that support transportation planning, traffic management, and commuter decision-making.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

This analysis answers the following questions:


1. What is the average commute time across Lagos corridors?
2. Which corridors experience the worst congestion?
3. What are the peak traffic periods?
4. Which days and hours have the highest congestion?
5. How is congestion distributed across trips?
6. How does weather impact commute times?
7. Which vehicle types are most affected by traffic?
8. What is the relationship between delays and commute time?
9. What operational factors contribute to congestion?
10. What actions can be taken to improve commuter experience?

## 💡 Key Insights

This analysis uncovered the following insights:

1. 70,000 trips were analyzed.
2. Average commute time was 34.41 minutes.
3. Longest commute recorded was 153.10 minutes.
4. Ajah–Victoria Island recorded the highest average commute time.
5. Peak congestion occurred around 6 PM.
6. Heavy Rain had the greatest impact on commute duration.
7. Weekday commute times were generally higher than weekends.
---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Below are the recommendations made from the analysis:

1.Introduce flexible work schedules.
2. Improve infrastructure on heavily congested corridors.
3. Implement weather-based traffic advisories.
4. Encourage alternative transportation options.
